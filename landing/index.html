PPangea

Your whole project,
bound like a book.

For filmmakers and every kind of creator. Plan it in one conversation, let the agent bind it into a living book — then read, mark it up in red ink, talk through your edits out loud, and watch the pages rewrite themselves. Your research, notes, and vision finally live in one place: the current state of the work.

Free. Local-first. Bring your own AI key. No account, no server, no lock-in.

You’re on the list — welcome aboard the Argo. ⚓

📖 A real bookshelf

Every project is a book on your shelf — title on the spine, your palette, your typeface. Open it to a two-page spread: the canvas on the left, your editor-in-residence on the right.

✒ Plan first, then bind

One prompt or a proper interview — the agent researches the best structure for your project and lays out 12–20 pages, images and all, before a word is drafted.

🖍 Read sessions

Grab the red pen, mark the page while you talk. Your ink and your voice are synced — end the session and the agent audits the page and rewrites it to your notes.

⚑ Notes that wait for you

Pin a thought to any page. Open notes surface on the cover as your task list, so you always know what still needs ironing out.

🎨 Draw on the images

Circle what’s wrong — “his ears need to be bigger” — and the image regenerates in place, on model, on palette.

🧠 Brain dump

Paste months of scattered notes. The agent reads the whole book and files every thought onto the right page.
